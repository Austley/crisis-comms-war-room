# Taylor Farms Crisis Case Notes

Last reviewed: July 30, 2026

## What Happened

The FDA and CDC investigated a multistate Cyclospora outbreak linked to shredded iceberg lettuce served at Taco Bell locations. FDA traceback converged on Taylor Farms de Mexico as the supplier of iceberg lettuce used by affected locations. Taylor Farms initiated a July 17, 2026 recall of iceberg lettuce sourced from central Mexico.

By the FDA's July 24 update, the outbreak involved 1,947 people reporting Taco Bell exposure across 9 states, with 98 hospitalizations and no deaths. A July 19 FDA update corrected a lab sample result as a false positive, but FDA later clarified that the false-positive sample did not change the outbreak investigation or the epidemiological basis for the recall.

## What Made It A Comms Crisis

- Public health harm was active and emotional.
- The source investigation was technical, evolving, and easy to misunderstand.
- Taylor Farms had to communicate across consumers, restaurants, retailers, regulators, employees, and media.
- A company statement opened with a regulator-focused vindication frame, which shifted attention from public safety to credibility.
- A false-positive test correction risked being interpreted as exoneration, even though the broader investigation and recall continued.

## Advisory Pattern To Encode

1. Lead with people affected and the practical safety action.
2. Stop arguing about attribution while the investigation is active.
3. Use regulator language precisely.
4. Separate confirmed facts from unknowns.
5. Put all updates in one public fact hub.
6. Create separate pathways for consumers, foodservice customers, employees, media, and regulators.
7. Commission independent review when internal credibility is depleted.
8. Treat trust recovery as a long program, not a single statement.

## Product Requirements Derived From This Case

- Incident intake that captures facts, unknowns, regulator posture, harmed stakeholders, and current response risk.
- Advisory scores for facts, empathy, and trust posture.
- Red-flag language detection for defensive or premature claims.
- Stakeholder-specific guidance.
- Draft assets: holding statement, employee note, media Q&A.
- Trust rebuild timeline.
- Source list and case file for transparent provenance.
